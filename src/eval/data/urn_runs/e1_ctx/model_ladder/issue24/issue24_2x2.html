Recall is quoted at the threshold whose realised false alarm rate is nearest 2%, and that realised rate is printed beside it because the three bucket score is coarse enough that no threshold lands exactly on the budget.

Source nature on top of three classes buys almost exactly what the seven class cut buys on its own. It moves AUC from 0.8519 to 0.8619, and the seven class model lands at 0.8620, so on separation alone the two are interchangeable.

They also stack. Seven classes crossed with source is the best of the four at 0.8690, and its gain over three classes, +0.0171 [+0.0091, +0.0237], is close to the sum of the two single gains, which says the class count and the source tier are carrying different information.

Recall at the 2% budget disagrees with that reading. The class count moves it from 34.0% to 42.2%, while source nature adds 1.5 points to three classes and 0.7 points to seven, so what actually reaches a flag comes from the finer flags and the source split only tidies the ranking further down.

The four tier split leans on thin cells, UNRELIABLE is about 0.6% of documents, and in the earlier transfer test the source tier gain did not carry across corpora, where the crossed model fell to 0.823 against 0.853 for three classes.

Source nature is four tiers, PRIMARY, RELIABLE, UNRELIABLE and UNRATED. Collapsing them to rated against unrated gives AUC 0.8629 for three classes and 0.8682 for seven.
