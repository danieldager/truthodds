What never reaches the scorer

Every recall number we publish is computed over claims that survived to scoring. A claim we never extracted is a miss the evaluation cannot see. This measures that hole against a post-level denominator: 10,500 community-noted posts, where a claim exists by construction because a human wrote a note about it.

Blind audit — how many drops are wrong?

The auditor sees the post text and nothing else: not the note, not our extracted claims, not which stratum the post came from. It answers one question — does this post contain a checkable factual claim? That is what separates a correct drop (the note was about an image, or about the account, or there was no claim) from a silent miss. In the third stratum a drop counts as wrong only if the blind claim was also absent from what we extracted. 400 posts sampled per stratum.

What this is and is not

The pool is community-noted English posts from the L3 acquitted set, so it over-represents contested content by construction and these drop rates are not a timeline estimate. Nothing in extraction or the gate was changed to produce them — this is a baseline on the shipped chain, measured before any tuning. The blind auditor is one model rather than a human panel, and its judgement of what counts as a checkable claim is the load-bearing assumption behind the miss estimate.
