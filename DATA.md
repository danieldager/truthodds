# Data

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the code in this repository only. The data the system was fitted and evaluated on is not included: the professional fact-check corpus, the claim sets drawn from it, the retrieved web pages and their reads, and third-party benchmarks such as AVeriTeC are not redistributed here, and each remains subject to its own licence and terms of use. The repository holds only aggregate metrics, fitted weights and figures derived from that data. Anyone reproducing the results needs to obtain the underlying sources themselves under their respective terms.
